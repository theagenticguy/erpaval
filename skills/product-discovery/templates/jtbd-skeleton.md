## Validation checklist

- [ ] Every job story follows "When X, I want Y, so I can Z" literally.
- [ ] Situation is specific (time, place, trigger), not demographic.
- [ ] Motivation describes a change in user state, not a feature.
- [ ] Outcome is observable.
- [ ] Every job story has at least one cited source.
- [ ] No persona-simulator job stories.
- [ ] At least 3 job stories (or explicit flag that source material didn't support 3).

When every job story is written and cited and validation is complete, flip `Status: IN PROGRESS` → `Status: COMPLETE`.
